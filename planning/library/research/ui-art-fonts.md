# Library categories 8 and 9: UI art icon families and fonts (first pass) (deep research, 2026-10-07)

Deep-research workflow: 101 Opus agents (5 search angles, fetch, 3-vote adversarial verification).

## Question

Find HUMAN-MADE, LICENSED UI art for StudPilot, which places UI into OTHER creators' commercial Roblox games. Target: at least 10 consistent icon FAMILIES (one creator or one pack each), 8,000 icons and 500 frames/buttons/bars/badges in total; plus fonts.
Priority: a glossy, cartoon, thick-dark-outlined 3D-style icon family of about 60+ game objects (coins, gems, eggs including patterned eggs, chests, gifts, potions, swords, pets, trophies, stars, hearts, lightning, rebirth, settings gear, shop basket, VIP crown, tickets, keys, locks, clocks), similar in look to Pet Simulator 99 or Bubble Gum Simulator Infinity icons.
Allowed licences only: CC0, CC-BY 3.0/4.0, licences that explicitly allow redistribution inside other people's games, or Roblox Creator Store public images by a single creator (used by asset ID). Banned: AI-generated art (check each pack's description, date and creator), NC, ND, "personal use", "no redistribution", most paid UI packs (BuiltByBit, Gumroad, itch paid), IP/brand characters, and ripped game assets.
Sources to check and expand: Kenney (Game Icons, UI Pack, Board Game Icons, Emotes, all UI packs); game-icons.net (CC-BY 3.0); OpenGameArt CC0/CC-BY icon and GUI packs; itch.io free CC0 packs (tag "icons", "gui"); Roblox Creator Store creators who publish whole icon sets as free images or decals (find specific creators and pack names); Poly Pizza / Quaternius 2D icons; fonts from Roblox's built-in font library and Google Fonts (OFL).
For each family report:
- the URL;
- the creator;
- the exact licence and licence URL;
- the icon count;
- the style (flat, glossy, outlined, 3D render, pixel);
- the resolution;
- evidence it is human-made;
- whether bulk download is allowed.
Rank the families by fit for a glossy outlined cartoon Roblox look. Give totals against the targets, and say clearly whether any free human-made family has glossy 3D eggs and pets.

## Summary

This run verified eight free, human-made-or-allowed-licence families, but none comes close to the targets and none is a glossy, thick-outlined 3D set. Only 4 are icon families: game-icons.net (CC-BY 3.0, flat monochrome, count not verified here), Kenney Game Icons (105, CC0), Kenney Emotes (CC0, 480 files) and Wenrexa UI Icons #1 (33, CC0). The other four are UI chrome packs: Kenney UI Pack Adventure (128 tiles, CC0), Kenney UI Pack (400+ sprites, CC0, explicit no-generative-AI statement), Free Buttons GUI Pack 1 (CC-BY 4.0) and SethByrd Mobile Game GUI Buttons (CC0 on the page, with a stale licence-file caveat). Verified totals are far below target: about 138 game-style icons are firmly counted (105 + 33) plus 480 emote files, against 8,000. Frames and buttons are roughly 128 firm plus 400+ Kenney sprites, whose count and classification were not confirmed, against 500. Families stand at 4 icon families (8 counting UI-chrome packs) against 10. No free human-made family with glossy 3D eggs and pets was found; the closest, Zxgly's Cartoony Roblox Vector Pack, is flat vector, has about 21 visible items, and its licence blocks edits, recolours and redistribution as an asset pack. The Roblox Creator Store route and the fonts were not resolved by this run.

## Verified findings

### game-icons.net is licensed CC-BY 3.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), which is allowed, but attribution is mandatory per original author (suggested form: 'Icons made by {author}. Available on https://game-icons.net'). StudPilot would need per-author credits inside every game it places icons in. Style is flat monochrome glyphs, so it is a poor fit for the glossy outlined cartoon look, and the icon total was not verified (a ~4,121-icon figure from the contributor list was refuted 0-3). Named human authors (e.g. Lorc, Delapouite) support the human-made requirement. Bulk download was not confirmed in this run.

- **Confidence:** high (vote 3-0 (licence), 3-0 (attribution), 0-3 (count, refuted))
- **Evidence:** Page states 'Creative Commons 3.0 BY license' and requires crediting the original author in your creation. Both licence claims passed 3-0. The icon-count claim was refuted 0-3, so no total is counted here.
- **Sources:** https://game-icons.net/about.html

### Kenney Game Icons (creator Kenney, OpenGameArt, 3 Nov 2014) is CC0 with 105 black-and-white PNG icons at 1x and 2x, with AI/SVG vector sources. Credit is optional. It is small (about 1.3% of the 8,000 target) and flat monochrome, so it does not match a glossy 3D look. The 2014 date and vector source files support human-made. Bulk download of the pack zip is allowed under CC0.

- **Confidence:** high (vote 3-0 (licence), 3-0 (count and style))
- **Evidence:** OGA page lists CC0 (http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), 105 icons, black and white, 1x/2x. Kenney's own page also lists CC0 and 105 icons.
- **Sources:** https://opengameart.org/content/game-icons

### Kenney Emotes Pack (OpenGameArt, 9 July 2018) is CC0, human-made (Kenney, long-established creator, 2018 predates generative AI, ships vector sources), 480 files per Kenney's page (480px PNGs, tilesheets, spritesheets), 16 flat or pixel emote styles. It is face emotes, not game-object icons, so it is a poor fit for the priority look and the file count should not be read as 480 distinct icons.

- **Confidence:** high (vote 3-0 (licence), 3-0 (human-made evidence))
- **Evidence:** OGA page shows CC0 and optional credit; Kenney's page shows CC0 and 480 files and commercial use allowed. Date and author confirmed 3-0.
- **Sources:** https://opengameart.org/content/emotes-pack, https://kenney.nl/assets/emotes-pack

### Kenney UI Pack - Adventure (OpenGameArt, 14 Aug 2024) is CC0, 128 separate PNG tiles plus spritesheets and vector source (Kenney's own page lists 130 files). It is a UI frame/button/panel/slider pack, so it counts toward the 500 frames/buttons target but not the icon target. Credit optional; bulk download allowed.

- **Confidence:** high (vote 3-0 (licence), 3-0 (count and classification))
- **Evidence:** OGA page lists CC0 (Creative Commons Zero) and '128 separate PNG tiles'. The breakdown of contents by item type came from a search summary rather than an itemised list on the page.
- **Sources:** https://opengameart.org/content/ui-pack-adventure, https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-pack-adventure

### Kenney UI Pack (kenney-assets.itch.io/ui-pack) is CC0 1.0 Universal, free for commercial projects without attribution, and the creator states 'No generative AI was used'. The page says over 400 sprites, but a claim that these come in 5 colour variants and count as frames/buttons toward the 500 target was refuted 0-3, so the exact frame count and classification are unconfirmed. Kenney's name and logo are reserved for official use.

- **Confidence:** medium (vote 3-0 (licence), 3-0 (no generative AI), 0-3 (count and classification, refuted))
- **Evidence:** Licence and no-AI statement each passed 3-0 from the creator's primary page. The sprite-count/classification claim failed 0-3.
- **Sources:** https://kenney-assets.itch.io/ui-pack, https://kenney.nl/support

### Wenrexa 'UI Icons Pack Free #1' (itch.io) is CC0 (Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal), 33 transparent 64x64 icons (zip 322 kB), with the creator's note 'No generative AI was used' (self-declared). The creator said in comments that the assets may be used in and sold inside games. Tiny (about 0.4% of 8,000) and low resolution, not a glossy 3D set. Wenrexa's other packs are not covered by this CC0 label and each needs its own check. No publication date on the page.

- **Confidence:** high (vote 3-0 (licence), 3-0 (33 icons), 3-0 (no-AI statement))
- **Evidence:** The page's licence field and the 'More information' table give CC0, 33 UI elements at 64x64, and the no-generative-AI line. The no-AI declaration scored medium confidence in verification because it is self-declared only.
- **Sources:** https://wenrexa.itch.io/icons1

### Free Buttons GUI Pack 1 by fakigame (OpenGameArt, 20 Oct 2018) is CC-BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) with attribution to payhip.com/fakigame required. It is buttons only (no icons), 512x512 transparent PNG, with an Illustrator source. Exact count is uncertain (claimed 46 in 4 colours, vote 1-2 so not confirmed). A restrictive License.txt in the zip was reportedly removed after commenters raised it; the current zip was not checked.

- **Confidence:** medium (vote 3-0 (licence), 1-2 (count, refuted))
- **Evidence:** Page licence field reads CC-BY 4.0; the 2018 date predates generative AI. The licence claim passed 3-0 but the 46-button count claim passed only 1-2.
- **Sources:** https://opengameart.org/content/free-buttons-gui-pack-1

### SethByrd 'Mobile Game GUI Buttons' (OpenGameArt, 26 Dec 2014) is listed CC0 and the author says no credit is needed, but a 2022 comment reported that the zip's license.txt used a custom no-redistribution licence. A moderator replied 'Fixed', but the current zip was not downloaded, so a clean licence is unconfirmed. Content is PSD buttons (a 38-button count was refuted 0-3), so it is a small buttons-only pack and not an icon family.

- **Confidence:** medium (vote 3-0 (licence), 0-3 (count and style, refuted))
- **Evidence:** Page licence field and CC0 URL confirmed 3-0 with a stated caveat on the zip's license.txt. The count and style claim failed 0-3.
- **Sources:** https://opengameart.org/content/mobile-game-gui-buttons

### Zxgly's 'Cartoony Roblox Vector Pack' (itch.io, released 13 Dec 2025, still in development) is a free Roblox-targeted vector pack, 256x256 PNG (zip about 2.8 MB) plus SVG hosted externally, with about 21 visible items by tag (animals, armor, coin, cash, key, potion, shield, sword, shop, settings and similar) and a stated no-generative-AI note. It is the closest in subject to the target, but it is flat vector, not glossy 3D, and no eggs were listed. Its licence is a custom itch.io one, and the claim that it allows commercial use was refuted 0-3. The verifier noted the licence bars editing, recolours and redistribution as an asset pack. Not countable as an allowed family until the licence text is read and cleared.

- **Confidence:** medium (vote 3-0 (description), 0-3 (licence suitability, refuted))
- **Evidence:** Descriptive facts (creator, free, 256x256, Roblox-targeted, no-AI note, no stated count) passed 3-0; the licence-permits-commercial-use claim failed 0-3.
- **Sources:** https://zxgly.itch.io/vector-icon-pack

### The Roblox Creator Store publishing docs give only uploader-side publishing rules (Open Use vs Restricted dependencies). They do not state the licence terms under which other creators may use Creator Store assets in their own commercial games, and they have no AI-generated-content rule or label, so human-made evidence must come from each creator's description, upload date and portfolio. Separately, two claims about Restricted vs Open Use privacy states (usable by any game by asset ID vs only by granted games) were each refuted 0-3, so the by-asset-ID usage route for Creator Store packs stays unresolved and no specific Creator Store creator or icon set was verified.

- **Confidence:** medium (vote 3-0 (no consumer licence terms), 2-1 (no AI rule), 0-3 and 0-3 (privacy claims, refuted))
- **Evidence:** Licence-terms gap confirmed 3-0 by direct fetch. AI-rule gap confirmed only 2-1, and the verifier relied on a summary of the page. Both privacy-state claims were refuted 0-3.
- **Sources:** https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/creator-store, https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/projects/assets/privacy.md

### Ranking by fit for a glossy outlined cartoon Roblox look, none of the verified families qualifies as a good fit. Best available by subject and style: (1) Zxgly Cartoony Roblox Vector Pack, the only cartoony Roblox-oriented set, but licence-blocked and flat; (2) Kenney UI Pack and UI Pack Adventure, the best fit for frames, buttons and bars (CC0, with an explicit no-generative-AI statement on the UI Pack); (3) Wenrexa #1 (colour UI icons, 64x64); (4) Kenney Game Icons and game-icons.net (flat monochrome); (5) Kenney Emotes (faces, off-target); (6) the two small button packs. No free human-made family with glossy 3D eggs and pets was found.

- **Confidence:** medium (vote synthesis (no direct vote))
- **Evidence:** Ranking is the synthesiser's inference from the verified style, resolution and licence facts above. Style claims were verified for Kenney Game Icons (monochrome) and Wenrexa (64x64 UI icons). Zxgly was verified as flat vector. No claim in the run established a glossy 3D set.
- **Sources:** https://zxgly.itch.io/vector-icon-pack, https://kenney-assets.itch.io/ui-pack, https://opengameart.org/content/ui-pack-adventure, https://wenrexa.itch.io/icons1, https://opengameart.org/content/game-icons, https://game-icons.net/about.html

### Totals against targets from what was verified: families 4 icon families (8 including UI-chrome packs) vs 10 target. Icons about 138 firm (Kenney Game Icons 105 + Wenrexa 33), plus 480 Kenney Emote files (faces, not objects), plus game-icons.net at an unverified count, vs 8,000. Frames/buttons about 128 firm (UI Pack Adventure) plus Kenney UI Pack 400+ (count unconfirmed), plus an unconfirmed 38 and 46 from the two button packs, vs 500. The 8,000-icon target cannot be met from the verified set; it would depend on game-icons.net (CC-BY with per-author credits, flat monochrome) once its real count is established.

- **Confidence:** medium (vote synthesis (no direct vote))
- **Evidence:** Arithmetic over claims verified 3-0 for counts (105, 33, 128). Counts for game-icons.net, the Kenney UI Pack sprites, and both button packs were refuted or unverified.
- **Sources:** https://opengameart.org/content/game-icons, https://wenrexa.itch.io/icons1, https://opengameart.org/content/ui-pack-adventure, https://kenney-assets.itch.io/ui-pack, https://game-icons.net/about.html

## caveats

Coverage is thin. The run did not verify any Roblox Creator Store creator or pack, any Poly Pizza/Quaternius 2D icons, any other OpenGameArt or itch.io icon pack beyond those listed, or the other Kenney packs the brief named (Board Game Icons, other UI packs). Fonts from Roblox's built-in library and Google Fonts (OFL) were not researched at all, so no font recommendation is supported. Several count claims were refuted or unconfirmed (game-icons.net total, Kenney UI Pack 400+ and its 5 colour variants, 38 and 46 buttons), so the frame and icon totals are floors, not estimates. Human-made evidence is mostly creator self-declaration or an old upload date, not independent audit. CC-BY packs (game-icons.net, fakigame) add a credit obligation to every game they are placed in. Licence status can change, and two button packs had licence-file disputes in comments that were not rechecked in the live zips. Bulk-download permission was inferred from CC0 or the licence text and not tested for each source; game-icons.net's bulk or API terms were not confirmed. The ranking is the synthesiser's judgment, since no style-fit claim was voted on.
