# StudPilot pricing (owner decisions, 2026-10-04)

## Unit
- **Credits**, charged by **real AI usage**, shown with two decimals.
- **1 credit = $0.05 of AI compute** (Workers AI cost, GLM 5.3 Flash). That is about 4,545 neurons at $0.011 per 1,000 neurons, or 150 of today's internal credits.
- The user sees an **estimate before** the build ("about 1.4 credits") and the **exact charge after** it ("1.37 credits").

**What builds cost** (measured, except where marked est.; convert at $0.05 per credit):

| Build | Cost | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Small | $0.03–0.07 | 0.52–1.40 |
| Typical | about $0.07 | about 1.40 |
| Big (systems, zones; up to 10 min) | $0.20–0.60 (est.) | 4–12 |

**Sources:**
- `R/2026-10-04-selfcheck.json`: objects cost 78–458 internal credits.
- `docs/autonomy/CURRENT_STATE.md`: a whole game cost about 1,000–1,800.
- `pricing.ts`: neuron price.

## Plans
| Plan | Price | Credits | About how many builds | Worst-case AI cost | Worst-case profit (after about 2.9% + $0.30 card fee) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 5 per day, max 30 per month | ~20 | $1.50 per user | – |
| Pro | $9.99 per month | 100 per month | ~70 | $5.00 | $4.40 |
| Max | $24.99 per month | 300 per month | ~200 | $15.00 | $8.97 |
| Top-up pack | $4.99 | 50 | ~35 | $2.50 | $2.04 |

- **Global cap on free spending:** a monthly pool that pauses free builds when it is used up. The amount is set from the measured cost per build, inside the owner's budget.
- **Charging starts** only after both: (1) every build type passes the blind-critic bar, and (2) an adult holds the Stripe account.
- **Competitors** (checked 2026-10-04):
  - Roblox Assistant and Lux: free.
  - Rebirth: $8.99/mo for 10 prompts a day; $15.99/mo for 25 a day.
  - Picoo: $19/mo for 200 credits; $49/mo for 500.

## Rules for the build
- **All numbers live in one config** (`packages/shared` plan limits plus `pricing.ts`), with a test that **recomputes the worst-case profit** for every plan and fails if any is below $0.
- **The estimate is honest.** If a build would go above its estimate by more than 50%, the user is told before it continues.
- **Failed or refused builds** are charged only for the work actually kept. Proposed rule; the planner must confirm with the owner.
- **Re-measure the real cost per build** at the cost milestone, then revisit the credit value and the plan sizes. These are estimates today.
- **Replace the old copy.** The pricing page ("~163 builds/month") and today's plan rows (Free 231/day, Pro 12,600/mo, Max 21,000/mo) are superseded.
