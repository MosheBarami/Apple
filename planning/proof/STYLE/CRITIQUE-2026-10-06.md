# StudPilot in-game UI: harsh critique

2026-10-06. Written by the planner as an adversarial review. The job was to find everything wrong, not to be fair.

## Verdict

The UI StudPilot builds looks like a **2012 "Web 2.0" button set laid over a Studio test place**. It is not a 2026 Roblox simulator. In order of damage, it has:
- glossy plastic shine bands;
- Lego-stud wallpaper on every surface;
- soft edges in the same hue as the fill, with no dark contour;
- gloomy navy window bodies;
- emoji and mismatched clip-art icons;
- the same yellow egg seven times.

The owner's words ("a cheap imitation, very flat, not trendy") are accurate, and the measurements below back them up.

**This is not one bad build.** The recipe itself is wrong, and three things kept it wrong for months:
- **A miscalibrated critic.** It gave "style 8" to the kit showcase you see below.
- **A reference board that never contained a top-grossing game.**
- **Planning documents (mine) that pointed at the wrong target.**

## 2. Measured: ours vs the top games

**Method:**
1. Crop the window area of each image.
2. Resize it to 480 px wide.
3. Run the script `ui-critique/ui-metrics.py` (it is in this folder):

   | Column | What it measures |
   |---|---|
   | Contour | Share of strong edges that have a dark pixel within 2 px: "is there a cartoon outline?" |
   | Edge | Mean edge strength: how crisp the image is |
   | White | Share of near-white pixels |
   | Dark | Share of dark pixels (V < 0.3) |

The crops are approximate and some images are JPEG.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| OURS StudKit pass 9 ("style 8") | **0.18** | 29 | 3.6 % | 12 % | FAIL |
| OURS U01 egg shop (X3) | **0.31** | 31 | 4.1 % | 16 % | FAIL |
| OURS U01 egg shop v2 (X4) | **0.53** | 26 | 2.1 % | **30 %** | FAIL |
| OURS U07 upgrades render | 0.77 | 28 | 2.2 % | 26 % | FAIL |
| OURS U14 gamepasses render | 0.81 | 33 | 2.3 % | 15 % | FAIL |
| OURS U05 daily render | 0.87 | 37 | 6.8 % | 17 % | FAIL (narrowly) |
| REF Pet Simulator 99 shop | 0.97 | **53** | **16.7 %** | 11 % | pass |
| REF Pet Simulator 99 pass cards | 0.94 | **53** | **14.6 %** | 11 % | pass |
| REF Bubble Gum Sim Infinity shop | 0.89 | **57** | **14.9 %** | 1 % | pass |
| REF Cartoony UI Kit, item shop | **1.00** | 44 | 9.9 % | 18 % | pass |
| REF Cartoony UI Kit, daily rewards | **1.00** | 52 | 10.2 % | 16 % | pass |

| REF Stud UI Pack, shop (premium studs) | 0.94 | 36 | 2.5 % | 13 % | pass |
| REF Cartoon stud shop | 0.91 | 34 | 2.0 % | 19 % | pass |

**What the numbers say:**
- **Every reference has a cartoon outline on 89–100 % of its strong edges.** Ours has it on 18–87 %. The "style 8" kit has it on **18 %**.
- **Ours is softer:** edge strength 26–37 against 34–57.
- **The gates separate the two groups cleanly** (contour ≥ 0.88, edge ≥ 34, dark ≤ 0.20 for light themes, stud pattern 1.5–3.5 %). All 9 references pass; all 6 of our builds fail.
- **Pattern strength** is the high-pass residual divided by the mean brightness. Our studs measure **4.6–5.6 %**; the Stud UI Pack's measure 1.7 % (header) and 3.4 % (cards).
- **What the gates can't do:** they set a floor, not quality. A build can pass them and still be ugly; that is what the critics and the owner's eye are for.

**Edge scans:** I took vertical pixel scans through buttons and card edges.
- **Pet Simulator 99 cards:** a solid **5 px near-black contour**.
- **The 2026 Cartoony kit buttons:** a **3–4 px dark navy contour** on top and **7 px** at the bottom (contour plus 3D lip).
- **Our "R$ 99" button and our cards:** **no dark pixel at all**, only mid-tones.

**Measured the Cartoony kit at native 1080p (`kit25_40.webp`):**
- **Window contour:** 6 px, about #0A1E36 (dark navy, not black).
- **Inside it:** a 5 px light rim (a pale tint of the face), then the face.
- **Window corner radius:** about 20 px.
- **Body:** a white (#F2FFFF) inset panel with its own 6 px navy contour.
- **Cards:** 5–6 px navy contour, a 5 px light rim, a gradient face, about 108 px tall, 17 px gaps.
- **Title:** white, about 42 px tall, 5 px navy stroke.

**Ours does none of this.** The kit (`apps/worker/src/studkit.ts`) draws:
- **border:** a 3.5 px border in the fill's own hue (line 129);
- **studs:** a stud texture at `ImageTransparency 0.3`, so 70 % visible (line 132);
- **shine band:** a translucent white band over the top 30 % (line 141);
- **shadow:** a black shadow at 60 % transparency (line 116), which makes a grey, muddy halo;
- **window body:** a navy #16233F (line 243).

## 3. Root causes, worst first

1. **The wrong recipe for every surface.** The face is built from:
   - a same-hue border;
   - a white translucent shine band;
   - a translucent lip;
   - a translucent black shadow;
   - full-strength studs.

   That is the Aqua/Web 2.0 gloss recipe. Modern cartoon UI uses a hard, dark contour, a light inner rim, a clean gradient, a solid darker lip, and patterns at 10–15 % contrast at most. Each critic pass added *another translucent layer* instead of questioning the recipe.
2. **Studs as the identity.** The stud texture is 70 % visible on headers, buttons, tabs, tiles, price pills, toasts and backgrounds. In the top games, panels carry **no studs at all**; they use subtle checker, rays or paw patterns. In the stud kits that do use studs (Stud UI Pack, Huge Clean Stud), the studs are a low-contrast texture inside a strong dark contour, not wallpaper.
3. **No dark contour, so no "cartoon".** Every reference draws one dark outline colour around *everything*: windows, cards, buttons, text, icons. That single rule creates the crisp sticker look. We use a different same-hue edge per element, so edges melt.
4. **Gloomy window bodies.** The navy #16233F body (dark pixels up to 30 % of the window) kills the candy palette. The top games use white or very light bodies, so the coloured cards pop.
5. **The icons are a ransom note.** The pack has 39 icons from 38 different uploaders, "picked by eye from 150 px thumbnails" (`icons.json`). The pack's own notes say "realistic-leaning", "more realistic than cartoon", "greyscale, not colourful" and "flatter than the others". Outline, lighting and perspective all differ. The scenes use **emoji** (🥚 🔥 🐚 🌌 ⚔️ ⏱️ 💰) inside white circles.
6. **The egg, the product's signature item, is the worst asset in the kit.**
   - **X3:** the same yellow egg image is used seven times.
   - **X4/X5:** a flat white egg decal tinted into flat ellipses. Its outline is jagged; `LATER.md` line 39 *already knew* ("outline is jagged at 100 px and up"), and it still shipped to the owner.
   - **The M5a render of U01:** the "eggs" are a shell, a fire emoji and a galaxy photo emoji.
7. **Text with no typographic system.** Outlines are same-hue: brown on yellow, dark green on green. The README admits numbers get "a 7 px outline that reads as a dark pill". Subtitles sit in brown sticker pills. Prices are typed as "R$ 99" and "Gems 12,500" instead of an icon and a number. "1x > 2x" uses a typed ">" character.
8. **Layout collisions, every time.**
   - **Overlaps:**
     - the header egg runs over the frame and the featured egg;
     - card eggs poke into the row above;
     - a pill crashes into a price button;
     - a toast overlaps the window (X4);
     - windows cover the HUD (U01, U08, U14 renders);
     - the tycoon bar overlaps the leftover pills (U15);
     - the menu title is cut in half (M5a U02 run).
   - **Empty space and clipping:** dead space fills 30–40 % of the window in U06, U09, U14 and X4; bottom rows are clipped with no scroll cue.
9. **No life.** The M5a U01 critique: "three frames are pixel-identical apart from button greying: the screen is dead". There are no rays, no shine sweep, no bob, no count-up in any capture.
10. **The template feel.** All 15 renders are the same skeleton: a coloured header bar with studs, a navy well, dark rows, emoji in white circles. A request for a main menu, a codes box or a rebirth panel gets the same window. The references share a style, but each screen has its own composition (hero card, grids, before→after, wheels, wide Day 7 card).
11. **It is staged in a Studio test, not a game.** Every capture sits on the default Baseplate: neon grid or grey floor, stock sky. All 15 renders also carry three leftover "Lobby / 0:30" pills from M3 piece S10, because nobody reset the place. The references are captured over a real, colourful world.
12. **The request is not met, and some content is fake.**
    - **Missing:** U10 has no avatars (the request asked for them). U01's "big featured egg" is a thin strip.
    - **Fake content:** the M5a U02 menu shipped rows saying "Coming soon" and "Placeholder", and its Shop is empty.
    - **Not the request:** the U04 rebirth panel is a spreadsheet of three text rows, not a before→after visual.

## 5. Process failures

1. **The critic was miscalibrated.** It saw only three references: a YouTube thumbnail of a paid kit (R01), one popup (R02) and one HUD (R04). It never saw a top-grossing game. It rated 7 eight times, then 8.
2. **The work was graded by its author.** The M5a renders were "judged by the orchestrating session", which means Claude Code graded its own work.
3. **The critic loop optimised words, not looks.** "Gloss is weak" was answered with more translucent layers. "Studs" was answered by making them louder (0.45 → 0.3 transparency). Nine passes went into polishing a wrong recipe.
4. **The capture was wrong.**
   - **Edit mode, not play.**
   - **The viewport size:** 1174×623, not a player's 1920×1080 or a phone.
   - **Guessed scaling:** outlines were doubled on a guess that "Studio captures at high DPI and halves them".
5. **The test place was dirty.** S10's pills sit in every render.
6. **Known ugly assets were parked in `LATER.md`.** The jagged egg, the weak icons, the faint gloss and the blob outlines all shipped to the owner anyway.
7. **There are no anti-pattern checks.** Nothing fails a build for:
   - emoji icons;
   - "R$" text;
   - currency typed as a word;
   - studs above 15 %;
   - translucent shine bands;
   - tinted full-colour icons;
   - overlaps;
   - clipped content without a scroll cue.

## 6. My own mistakes as the planner

- **The Style Bible picked the wrong anchor.**
  - **The master reference:** a YouTube thumbnail of a paid "Studs-Style UI" kit (R01), not the top games.
  - **Studs as a must-have:** "the stud texture" was made one of the seven required signatures, so missing studs counted as a failure.
  - **"Same-hue borders":** the Bible told Claude Code to draw them.
- **The handoff (M5a, line 295) said "dark-blue translucent body".** That is the navy well. It also said "outer black strokes ≈5", which conflicted with the Bible; Claude Code followed the Bible.
- **The pass rule was too lenient.** "Style ≥ 8 with one signature weak" let pass 9 through.
- **The colour gate measures colourfulness, not quality.** Pass 9 passed it with saturation 0.54. A loud, bad UI passes a saturation gate.

All of these are replaced by `STUDPILOT-UI-SPEC-v2.md`.

## 7. What "trendy" means in 2026: the 10 laws (from about 290 downloaded references)

1. **One dark contour colour for everything** (very dark navy or near-black): 5–6 px on windows, 4–6 px on cards, 3–4 px on buttons, 3–5 px text strokes at 1080p.
2. **A light inner rim inside every contour** (3–6 px, a pale tint of the face): the "bevel" that makes things feel 3D without gloss bands.
3. **White or very light window bodies.** Colour lives on cards, buttons and headers.
4. **Gradient faces with 2–3 stops,** sometimes with a hue shift (yellow→orange, lime→green). Never a white translucent band on top.
5. **Solid 3D lips on buttons:** a darker shade of the face, 4–7 px under the button. Pressing moves the face down and the lip shrinks.
6. **Quiet patterns at 10–15 % contrast:** sunburst rays behind featured items, a checker or diagonal pattern in headers, theme patterns (paws, bubbles). Studs only as a faint texture inside a strong contour, never as wallpaper.
7. **Big, consistent, rendered icons.** One art style (the same lighting, outline and perspective), 35–55 % of the card height, often breaking the card edge. Never emoji.
8. **Real currency glyphs:** the Robux glyph (`\u{E002}` in a TextLabel) or a gem or coin icon, then the number with thousands separators. Never "R$" or "Gems 100".
9. **One clear hero per screen:** a big featured card (rainbow or rays, "NEW!" or "BEST VALUE" badge), then a tidy grid. Rarity colours follow player convention: grey, green, blue, purple, gold, red or rainbow.
10. **Always alive:** rays rotating slowly behind the hero, a shine sweep every few seconds, hover 1.05, press 0.95, a pop-in on open, numbers counting up.

## 8. What will NOT fix it (don't do these)

- More translucent gloss layers, louder studs, thicker same-hue outlines.
- More critic passes against the same three references.
- Better wording in the reply.
- More free icons picked by eye from thumbnails.
- Shipping a known-ugly asset and noting it in `LATER.md`.
