## 5. What is policy rather than code

1. "We never sell your data." No code can show a negative.
2. Roblox data is never used for AI training. The mechanism is that nothing reads it; the commitment is the owner's and Roblox's Third-Party App Policy (external).
3. Improvement data: anonymised, opt-out, excluding Roblox data, an Open Cloud key, credentials and payment data. Nothing is collected, so there is nothing to test beyond the closed gate.
4. "Before collection starts we will tell every account holder: by email, and in the app for accounts with no email address." **The in-app notice for Roblox-only accounts does not exist** (such an account's address is `@users.studpilot.invalid`, which nothing can deliver to). The pages now say so in the future tense and admit it is not built. **It must be built before the training gate may open:** `tests/promises-match-the-product.test.mjs` requires, for `CUSTOMER_WORK_TRAINING_ENABLED` to be true, both that the gate reads `improvement_opt_out` and that a `policy_notice` kind exists in the worker's `NOTIFICATION_KINDS` (the inbox is the one in-app channel that reaches every account; if the notice is built another way, re-aim that check in the same change). The same notice is needed before the first significant policy or terms change for any account with no email address.
5. 13 and older, "Roblox's sign-in service is for 13+" (external), and "an account found to belong to someone under 13 is deleted on report" (operational: an operator removes the sign-in identity, which the app cannot do). **"Google and Discord already require 13 and older" was removed in 2.3 (section 9, A4): it is an external claim about those providers' rules that nothing here shows, and Google offers supervised accounts to children under 13. ### Decisions taken in this cycle, for the owner to see

1. **C1-0: the account is found by a pointer, not by a link request** (above). The pointer is the same keyed one-way code the wipe already leaves on the Supabase account, next to the account id in a new D1 table, `roblox_wiped(code, user_id)`: it is on both pages (the Roblox data list, the loss paragraph), in the export, and deleted with the account and when the person signs in again. It is created by the worker on first use; nothing is applied by hand.
2. **C1-5: put the link back, rather than reword the pages to say support finishes a sweep that failed after the account went.** The pages and `erasure.ts` state the stronger order. The one residue is the double failure (the account cannot be removed and the put-back fails).
3. **C1-9: the broader reading of "inconclusive":** any answer from Roblox that cannot be acted on raises the event, not only a night where every grant was unanswered. A 4xx for ONE token among answered ones therefore raises it every night until it is looked at.
4. **The migration.** `infra/supabase/migrations/0015_usage_ledger_survives_account_deletion.sql` is unchanged, and is still to be applied by hand, BEFORE the Worker that deletes sign-in identities is deployed. This cycle needs nothing more from Supabase. It needs two things from D1, both made by the Worker itself on first use: the `created_username` column and the `roblox_wiped` table.

### Still open after this cycle

- The double failure in C1-5 (Auth cannot remove the account, and the link cannot be put back).
- The first live runs: the Roblox introspection call (9.4, items 1 to 5), and the two Cloudflare list calls and the `limit` (9.3). Until then the mocks encode a reading of the documentation.
- An account made by a sign-up that stopped halfway and was finished later is given `created_username` from the display name Auth holds at that moment. If the person had changed that display name in between (they could not have signed in to do it, so this is not expected), the wipe would compare only the current username, as it did before this cycle.
