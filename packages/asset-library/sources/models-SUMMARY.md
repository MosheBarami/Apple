# models.jsonl summary

Catalog of free 3D model and map sources for bright low-poly cartoon simulator and tycoon games on Roblox.
File: `packages/asset-library/sources/models.jsonl`. It has **526 entries** (426 import-ok, 100 reference-only), deduped by normalized URL.
Nothing was downloaded, no accounts were created, no logins were made and no forms were submitted. Licences come from the source pages.

## Licence rules applied
- **import-ok** covers CC0 or public domain (Kenney, Quaternius, KayKit, Poly Haven, CC0-filtered itch, CC0 OpenGameArt), free Roblox Creator Store assets (Roblox Terms of Use), and explicit commercial-reuse licences. CC-BY (Sketchfab, some Poly Pizza) needs attribution. MIT and Apache need the licence notice kept.
- **reference-only** covers BuiltByBit and ClearlyDev marketplace licences, unconfirmed Sketchfab licences, YouTube tutorials, fan copies of commercial games (Bee Swarm style maps) and leaked-game repos (IP risk).
- Poly Pizza entries marked "CC0 or CC-BY 3.0" need a per-model check on import. Credit the author if it is CC-BY.
- Creator Store free models may contain scripts. Strip or audit every Script and LocalScript before use (backdoor risk).

Suggested hub build order:
1. Nature layer: Kenney Nature Kit, Quaternius Stylized Nature and KayKit Forest.
