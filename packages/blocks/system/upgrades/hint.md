Upgrades with levels, bought on the server: ReplicatedStorage.UpgradeBuy(name) -> ok, message. Level n+1 costs
cost x growth^n; refused at max or without the money. Levels are saved; Upgrades.level(player, name) is what earning
or movement scripts read to apply the effect. Player attributes Upgrade_<name without spaces>_Level and _Cost feed a
row-list (the row's label must equal the name). Needs the currency block.
