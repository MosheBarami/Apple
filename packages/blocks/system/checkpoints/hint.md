Saves obby progress: parts in Workspace.<folder> named by number (1, 2, 3 or Stage1, Stage2...) are checkpoints.
Touching the next one (or any later one with skipAhead) sets the player's Stage (leaderstats and saved), and the player
respawns standing on the checkpoint of their stage. The checkpoint parts themselves are built by a prop or zone block
or the builder. Needs the currency block (for saving).
