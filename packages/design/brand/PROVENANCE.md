# Provenance of the StudPilot mark

**The mark was drawn in this repository by Claude Code, as vector path data, on 2026-10-05, for
handoff M2 step 2.1.** It was not generated by an AI image service, traced from a render, or taken
from a stock library. No network call was made to make it.

## What it is

A rounded tile, the plate a brick stands on, with one circular stud knocked out of it and one corner
left sharp. The sharp corner points up and to the right: forward, the heading of a pilot. It is one
filled path (`studpilot-mark.svg`), 32 by 32 units, painted with `currentColor`, so it takes the
accent wherever it is placed and works on any ground.

- **Geometry.** The tile spans 3 to 29 on both axes. Three corners have a radius of 9; the top right
  has 1.2. The stud is a circle of radius 4.6 centred at (15.4, 17), a little below and left of
  centre so the sharp corner does not pull the shape off balance. Tile and stud are one path with the
  even-odd rule, so the stud is a real hole and shows whatever is behind the mark.
- **Why it reads at 16 px.** There is one shape and one hole. At 16 px the tile is 13 px wide and the
  stud 4.6 px across; nothing else exists to be lost. The earlier marks were outlines and thin
  strokes that closed into grey rings at that size.
- **The wordmark** (`studpilot-wordmark.svg`) is the mark followed by "StudPilot" set as SVG text in
  the system font stack, weight 600. It uses `currentColor` throughout. Because it is live text it
  takes whatever system face the viewer has; nothing is downloaded.

## What was made from it

`node scripts/make-brand-assets.mjs` renders `favicon.svg` (the mark in the default accent from
`tokens.css`, on a tile in the dark base colour) and the PNG icons `icon-16.png`, `icon-32.png`,
`icon-180.png` and `icon-512.png` from it, copies the site's set into `apps/site/public/`, and
renders the share card `apps/site/public/og.png` from `apps/site/brand/og.html`. `--check` fails if
any of them is out of date with its source.

## What it replaced

Three earlier drawings were in production at once, and all three were deleted in the same change:
an azure isometric brick (the favicon, the icon set and the share card), a folded-sheet outline (the
site header and footer, the web app's favicon and the assistant avatar), and a hexagon holding an
isometric cube (the app rail, the sign-in screens and the empty-state illustrations).
`packages/design/src/web/brand.test.mjs` fails if any of their geometry or files comes back.
