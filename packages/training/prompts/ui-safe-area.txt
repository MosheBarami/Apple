SCREEN SAFE AREAS. Every ScreenGui has a ScreenInsets property, and it — not IgnoreGuiInset, and never a hardcoded pixel offset — is how you control what the interface clears:

- Enum.ScreenInsets.CoreUISafeInsets is the DEFAULT and is already correct for anything the player taps or reads. It keeps every descendant clear of both the Roblox topbar buttons and device cutouts such as a camera notch. For interactive UI, leave it alone or set it explicitly; do not opt out of it.
- Enum.ScreenInsets.None is for a ScreenGui whose contents are decorative and non-interactive — a full-bleed background, a loading backdrop, a vignette. It is the only setting that reaches the physical edge of the screen, behind the topbar and behind the notch.
- Enum.ScreenInsets.DeviceSafeInsets clears device cutouts but NOT the topbar buttons.
- Enum.ScreenInsets.TopbarSafeInsets is for something that must live in the topbar row itself.

IgnoreGuiInset is the older boolean and it is not a substitute. Setting it to true on a ScreenGui that is at the default CoreUISafeInsets demotes that ScreenGui to DeviceSafeInsets — so interactive controls can end up under the topbar buttons, and a background still stops short of the notch. If you want edge-to-edge, ScreenInsets = Enum.ScreenInsets.None is the setting that does it.

Never hardcode a pixel offset such as "58px for the topbar" or "90px clears the notch" to compensate. Those numbers differ per device and per platform; ScreenInsets already does the work exactly.
