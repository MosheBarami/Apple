# Viral Roblox hits 2023-2026: case studies and cross-cutting patterns
_Researched 2026-10-04 by Claude research agent (Sonnet 5.5). Sources: 68 (see list; [S45]-[S47] and [S68] are grouped sources)._
_Gap pass 2026-10-04: 17 items resolved, 8 still open._

Scope note: the live web-search budget of the first session ran out partway through, so the late 2026 items rest on fetched pages and on search-result summaries; a gap pass the same day added [S48]-[S68]. Anything resting only on a search-result summary or a community wiki is tagged "unverified" or "fan wiki". All CCU numbers are peaks of concurrent users (CCU) unless stated. Rolimons numbers [S45] are third-party, fetched 2026-10-04, and Roblox began a bot-account clean-up on 2026-08-19 [S36], so the newest peaks may later be revised.

## Key facts

### Platform context (why 2025 was different)
- Roblox's platform-wide peak concurrency rose from 13.9M (April 2025) to 30.6M (June 2025) [S4], to an all-time high above 32M reported in the Q2 2025 letter [S8], then to a reported 47.4M in August 2025 [S5]; Roblox itself cites "45 million" on an August weekend [S37][S38]. Third-party framing: the 47.4M day was the "admin war" between the Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot developers [S5][S2].
- Daily active users (DAU): 85.3M (Feb 2025, [S43]), 151.5M avg in Q3 2025 (+70% YoY) [S7], 144M in Q4 2025, 132M in Q1 2026, 123M in Q2 2026 [S39][S10][S11]. Roblox says each of the three biggest 2025 hits (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, 99 Nights in the Forest) hit CCU peaks that would have exceeded the whole platform's peak a year earlier [S7].
- Roblox's own Q2 2026 letter says bookings grew only 8% YoY to $1.557B and Q3 2026 bookings are guided to fall 14-18% YoY, partly because engagement shifted from "high monetizing, 2025-vintage viral games" to new and evergreen games that monetise less per hour [S11]. The top 10 experiences were about 30% of hours three years ago and about 20% now [S11][S39]. Lesson: 2025-style mega-viral titles were unusually good at monetising; the platform is now flatter.
- Roblox is changing the discovery objective toward 28-day retention (tests "at scale" from Q2 2026) [S10], and says discovery signals such as playthrough rate and 7-day play days per user are shared with creators [S38]. New and novel games get a curated "Standout Games" home-screen section with video previews [S10].
- Creator economics: DevEx rate rose 8.5% on 2025-09-05 (from $3.50 to $3.80 per 1,000 Earned Robux) [S7]; top 1,000 creators averaged about $1.3M on a trailing basis, up over 50% YoY [S9]; from 2026-06-08 effective creator earnings on in-game spend by age-checked over-18 US users rise to 37.8% from 26.6% [S10]; a further DevEx rate increase landed in June 2026 "to incentivize novel game creation" and DevEx fees were $363M in Q2 2026 [S11]. Details of rates belong in file 08; they matter here because they explain why studios now chase novel, high-retention games.
- Age checks hurt engagement: 45% of DAU age-checked by 2026-01-31 [S9]; 51% globally by end of Q1 2026 [S10]. Roblox Kids (under 9) and Roblox Select (9-15) tiers appeared in June 2026 [S43].

### Case studies

#### Grow a Garden (launched 2025-03-26; Roblox's letter says 2025-03-25) 
- Core loop: buy seeds with Sheckles, plant on plots, crops grow in real time even while you are offline, harvest and sell, buy rarer seeds, hatch pets; max 4 players per server [S1][S45]. The pitch Splitting Point's Janzen "Jandel" Madsen gave: everyone likes to farm and to watch something grow, and you come back to bigger plants [S12].
- Origin: built in about three days by an anonymous 16-year-old (BMWLux) who kept 50% of the game; Madsen found it at about 500 CCU per his own interview [S12] (Wikipedia says about 1,000 CCU when Splitting Point bought its share in April 2025 [S1]), partnered, and his team shipped weekly live updates; Do Big Studios took a minority share (reported as bought at the end of May 2025 [S63]) and helped expand it [S1][S12].
- Growth timeline [S1][S12][S40]: about 100K CCU in week 2; 1M CCU about a month after launch; fastest game to 1B visits (33 days); 5M CCU on 2025-05-17/18 (Blood Moon update); 8.9M on the 2025-05-24 weekend [S40]; 11.7M on 2025-05-31 (Bizzy Bees); 16M+ on 2025-06-14 (Working Bees), breaking Fortnite's 15.3M record from 2020; 21.3M on 2025-06-21 (Summer Update); Guinness-certified 21.6M [S4]; Roblox's letter says nearly 22M in July [S8]; all-time peak about 22.3-22.4M on 2025-08-23 [S1][S45]. Peak DAU about 60M and 30B plays in 3-4 months [S12].
- The hook: a thumbnail-readable idea (garden), passive progress that rewards returning, a visible rarity ladder (exotic plants visible to everyone on the server), and a weekly event rhythm. Stealing/griefing was optional in v1 (Robux "crop theft" perk [S1]); v2 made night raids and guilds the centrepiece [S29].
- Spread: Roblox's Q2 2025 letter credits AI-driven discovery plus the curated "Today's Picks" sort; 75%+ of its DAU also played another experience that day [S8]. Altman Solon found the game was over two-thirds of Roblox's roughly 30M concurrent players at the June peak [S6]. GameDiscoverCo attributed growth to organic interest and rejected bot-inflation claims [S40]; TD Cowen's bot allegation was later retracted and Roblox said its analysis showed genuine popularity [S1].
- Monetisation: Robux to speed growth, perks, weekly exclusive items that require you to be online to claim, pets from loot boxes; Wikipedia says DoBig Studios was criticised for excess monetisation [S1]. It topped Roblox's Top Earners within a month of launch, faster than any prior experience [S8].
- Revenue (third-party, estimates, not Roblox figures): about $12M from Robux sales in May 2025, attributed to CreatorExchange analytics and repeated by Calcalist on 2025-07-13 [S57]. A model-based site puts lifetime revenue at about $286.9M (2026-10-03 data) using fixed assumptions (2% engagement, 150 Robux average session spend, 70% developer share, $0.0038 per Robux) applied to 36B visits [S58]; that is arithmetic on visits, not observed earnings. Roblox publishes no per-game revenue [S8].
- Cadence: weekly updates; two dedicated event teams created July 2025 [S1]. Team: one teenager plus Splitting Point; after July 2025 multiple teams (exact headcount unverified).
- Ownership and the "dispute" (resolved as: no dispute or lawsuit is documented): BMWLux 50%, Splitting Point (bought in April 2025), Do Big minority [S1][S63]; percentages beyond BMWLux's 50% are undisclosed [S65]. In 2026 Jandel's Discord role changed from "owner" to "developer", he posted a farewell thanking fans for "these last 6 months", and he kept working on Plants & Brainrots [S63]; a lower-trust explainer says Jandel and DJ Jhailatte stepped back from day-to-day work earlier in 2026 and BMWLux (reported under the name Adrian) now runs the original game, while Jandel revealed Grow a Garden 2 on 2026-05-31 [S64]. None of [S1][S63][S64][S65] reports a legal or ownership conflict; the January 2026 "drama" date from the first pass could not be verified and is dropped.
- Aftermath: still 35.9B visits [S45] but only about 140-158K CCU by mid-2026 [S29][S45]. Sequel Grow a Garden 2 launched 2026-06-12 by Strawberreh Squad, 400K+ CCU in 20 minutes [S29], peaked under 1M [S45][S47], and by August had dropped to rank 8 (171.8K CCU, -66% from its month high) [S32]. A film is in development with Story Kitchen (announced 2025-11-05) [S1][S18]. Grow a Garden 2 is credited to the group Strawberreh Squad; one explainer (Nerdschalk, in [S68]) calls it a separate experience rather than a sequel by the original team, while another [S64] says Jandel and DJ Jhailatte worked on it: who builds it is unresolved.

#### Steal a Brainrot (launched 2025-05-16)
- Core loop: you have a base; a conveyor delivers voxel-style "Brainrot" characters (Italian-brainrot memes) you buy with cash; each earns passive income per second; other players can steal them from your base (capture-the-flag style) so you buy a temporary shield, locks and gear; rebirth resets for multipliers; best items and "admin" powers cost Robux [S2].
- Maker: Sam Brakta (SpyderSammy), Chief Product Officer of DoBig Studios, under the group BRAZILIAN SPYDER; it grew from the "Steal a ..." format (fan wikis say he bought the concept from the author of "Steal a Character"; not confirmed by any fetched source) [S2][S68]. Team and build time: Wikipedia gives neither [S2]; fan wikis name a small core (SpyderSammy, a co-owner, a QA lead and a producer) with DoBig staff behind them, headcount unknown [S68]. No developer interview giving build time was found. Roblox showcased the game at GDC 2026 [S51].
- Timeline [S2][S3][S5]: 5M CCU and 7B visits by July 2025; 20M on 2025-08-23 (admin war with Grow a Garden); 24M on 2025-09-13 (Extinct Event) passing Grow a Garden; first game over 25M in early October 2025; Rolimons all-time peak 25,836,222 [S45]. Roblox says it reached this four months after launch [S9].
- What spread it: the public feud between Jandel and Sammy in August 2025 ("whether it was real beef or marketing genius, it worked" [S5]); tens of millions of TikTok/YouTube views of steal tips and clips of kids crying after theft [S2]; "admin abuse" events where developers hand out rare items live, which push streamers and players to log in simultaneously [S2][S27]; licensed Fortnite Creative clone "Steal the Brainrot" hit 1,019,725 CCU [S41] (Epic licensed it in July 2025 [S2]).
- Events: Bruno Mars concert 2026-01-17: 12,862,161 concurrent viewers (Guinness record for a single artist in a videogame), under 8 minutes, 5,428,644 limited "Brunito Marsito" brainrots delivered, 10M livestream views [S21][S22][S23]; Dexerto counted 12,712,359 at its snapshot [S22]. It beat Travis Scott's 2020 Fortnite event (12.3M) [S21].
- Monetisation: dual currency; Robux-only best items; criticised as pay-to-win by Polygon's Patricia Hernandez [S2]. Cheating/automation scripts widely reported [S2]. Revenue (third-party estimate only): a CreatorExchange post put July 2025 at about $11M for the month (+117%, 720M hours; seen only as a search-result snippet of an X post [S68]). Roblox publishes no per-game figure; a model-based lifetime figure from a calculator site is arithmetic on visits and is not used here. In 2026 the game won Best Innovation in a Branded Game (Steal a Brainrot x GOAT) [S48].
- Legal/IP: Tung Tung Tung Sahur character pulled in September 2025 over a licensing dispute, restored 2025-11-29 after licensing was resolved; October 2025 lawsuit against a Fortnite Creative copycat; film in development with Story Kitchen and DoBig [S2].
- Decay: 25.8M peak to a 30-day peak of 1.68M by fall 2026 [S45]; average session about 11.4 min, roughly a third of Grow a Garden's playtime per visit in Sept 2025 (reported by PocketGamer: "average playtime approximately one-third" [S3]).

#### 99 Nights in the Forest (launched June 2025; surged from July)
- Core loop: co-op survival (up to 25 per server) day and night: gather logs/metal in the day, build a camp, craft, defend the fire at night against escalating waves; goal 99 nights to rescue four missing children; "just one more run" countdown [S26][S45].
- Makers: Grandma's Favourite Games, three New Zealand creators (Alec Kieft game design, Matthew Hufton programming, Cameron Angland environments) [S18][S24][S47]. Won Best Adventure and Best Horror at the 2025 Innovation Awards [S49] and Best Survival Game in 2026 [S48]. Hufton spoke on a GDC 2026 developer panel and Kieft on a culture-trends session [S50].
- Numbers: 137M daily visits over 2025-09-01 to 09-14 vs Grow a Garden's 108M; likely the first survival game to sustain 1M+ average CCU [S26]; 14,153,173 peak CCU on 2025-10-04 [S45]; Roblox lists it among the three games that each exceeded the previous year's platform peak [S7]. 
- What spread it: co-op chaos that makes funny clips, "scary but funny" tone; a base-building YouTube video with over 9M views and a TikTok over 45M views [S26]; weekly themed content drops with new monsters, classes and badges [S26].
- Afterlife: 26B visits by April 2026 [S18]; 152K CCU on 2026-08-20 (rank 10) [S32]; film at 20th Century Studios announced April 2026 with the three devs as executive producers [S24][S18]; Fortnite collaboration August 2026 [S18].

#### Plants vs Brainrots (August 2025) and Escape Tsunami for Brainrots (2026-01-05)
- Plants & Brainrots (Splitting Point group, [S45]): lane-based tower defense merging Grow a Garden's seeds, Steal a Brainrot's memes and Plants vs Zombies; rebirth gives permanent +50% Luck and Money per tier; weekly "admin abuse" events; later a card/deck modifier update [S27]. Peak 8,654,525 on 2025-10-11 [S45][S27]; 30-day peak only 37K in fall 2026 [S45].
- Escape Tsunami For Brainrots (Wave of Brainrots, a Do Big Studios label; created 2025-12-15, public January 2026, [S28][S47]): run across a map to grab Brainrots before tsunami waves, bring them home for passive income; rare brainrots as flex items; admin events and leaderboards; peak 4,996,032 (fifth-highest ever at the time) [S45][S28]. 
- Pattern: both are the same "brainrot" skin on a proven sub-loop. Mobilegamer.biz observed that developers prioritise audience connection and live-service Discord management over polish [S25].

#### Steal An Egg (launched 2026-07-25) and the "steal" genre
- Loop: steal eggs, hatch pets, earn from pets, upgrade base and treadmill, then steal more (7 players per server) [S31][S33][S45]. A reviewer's explanation: small servers turn theft "from an anonymous number into a grudge" [S32].
- Numbers: about 543K average CCU in its first full month (August), 14,293,524 peak in September 2026 [S31][S45]. A tracker snippet gives a different September peak (13,844,751 on 2026-09-18) [S68]; trackers disagree by about 3%.
- Developer: the group "and Collect Rare Pets" (ID 825735094), whose name is built so the game card reads "Steal An Egg and Collect Rare Pets"; the owner account is misfitsbsthree (display name "ben") according to a fan guide that cites the Roblox API of 2026-09-04 [S60]; one search summary named a different owner (Badr_Omega): unresolved. Team size and build time: not disclosed in any source found [S60][S61].
- Real players vs bots: before the removal on Monday 2026-08-24 the game had over 500K concurrent players, which the trade press described as players choosing to run the video-feed treadmill [S61]. Roblox's bot clean-up post (2026-08-19) named no games and gave no counts [S36]; the only games forum users named as heavily botted were Murder Mystery 2 and Pet Simulator 99 [S36], and MaxPowerGaming said the bot clean-up possibly explained Grow a Garden 2's decline and did not say that of Steal An Egg [S31]. So no source says the 14.29M peak was botted and no source can show what share was real: the real-player share is unknown.
- Controversy: its treadmill fed an AI-generated short-video "Reels" feed; Roblox pulled the game briefly in late August 2026 and then barred games from Roblox Kids and Select that combine a media feed, auto-scroll and a reward for continued watching [S31][S47][S61]. The game returned without the feed [S31].

#### +1 Speed Keyboard Escape and the "+1 Speed" genre (2026)
- SecretVerse Studio. Loop: each step on a candy/chocolate keyboard key adds speed; faster speed clears 13 obstacle stages; wins buy multipliers (trails, auras); rebirths reset speed for permanent multipliers [S35]. Free x1 AFK treadmill, paid Diamond (x9) and Admin (x100) treadmills [S35]. 
- Peak 7,263,327 on 2026-07-18 [S45]; other write-ups cite 6.2M in August and 5.62M [S34][S47]: unresolved. Ranked #3 (358K) on 2026-08-20 with a copycat "+1 Speed Monkey Escape" at #7 [S32].
- Why it worked, per reviewers: nothing to learn, constant visible progress, ASMR keyboard click sounds, strong thumbnail/video readability [S34][S35].

#### Animal Hospital (Animal Anomaly, 2026-05-10)
- Loop: night-shift nurse; check in animal patients, treat them with the right medicine, spot which patients are secretly anomalies; a sanity meter drops on anomaly encounters; three patient deaths end a shift; money and Animal Coins reward; a permanent shift-streak counter [S30]. Up to 30 players per server [S45].
- Developer: the group Animal Anomaly, owned by the developer Roytt; Tubefilter's report on Roblox's 2026 developer video series describes Roytt as a solo developer who later founded a studio with friends [S53]; a fan wiki lists a co-developer (Kruzsky) plus two staff admins and seven testers [S68]: team size is therefore unclear. It swept the 2026 Innovation Awards: Best New Game, People's Choice, Best Innovation in Creative Direction and the Builderman Award [S48].
- Numbers: 57K CCU in the first month, 580K+ by 2026-06-28, 736,948 on 2026-07-05 [S30]; Rolimons peak 1,273,105 on 2026-07-10 (the day of the "Harlow's Favorite" update) [S45][S68]; Rolimons also shows 2.38B visits and about 12 minutes average playtime [S68]; 358.5K on 2026-08-20 and it was Roblox's #1 game on the recommendation system by placements [S32]; -62% month on month in August [S31]. Named by Roblox as a new top title in the Q2 2026 letter [S11].
- Monetisation: Animal Coins sold for Robux; classes unlock via coins or Robux; consumables (tasers, coffee) [S30].

#### Kick a Lucky Block (No More Flops, 2026-04-07)
- Roblox tycoon-style brainrot kicker; 1,691,570 peak CCU; 1.9B visits; max 5 players; avg 11 minutes [S45][S47]. Named in Roblox's Q2 2026 letter with Animal Hospital and Grow a Garden 2 as titles that went "from initial release to top experiences" [S11]. Little else verified.

#### Murder Mystery 2 (Nikilis, 2014): an evergreen's record in 2026
- Social deduction (murderer, sheriff, innocents), 12 players; Rolimons shows an all-time peak 1,352,075 on 2026-08-16, driven by the Summer Event 2026 (2026-07-23 to 08-23: new Pier map, daily quests, cumulative rewards, limited weapons) [S45][S31][S32]. Analysts noted the drop right after Roblox's bot-account enforcement and suggested botted farming of the event [S31]: unverified.

#### Dead Rails (RCM Games, early 2025)
- Loop: up to 4-player co-op on a steam train through a 1899 zombie desert, burning fuel (anything, including corpses) to keep the train moving, buying gear, class system, ending at 80,000 metres [S14][S13]. Bonds are the earned/purchased premium currency [S14]. Procedural terrain, a moving base and class-based co-op were technically unusual [S47].
- Makers and dates: fan wikis credit two developers, RiccoMiller (owner of RCM Games) and thememermonkey, and give a release of 2025-01-01 with a first-place chart position on 2025-03-22 [S68]; no developer interview or build time was found, and Roblox's own ownership or team data is absent. It won Best Use of Tech and Best Strategy Experience at the 2025 Innovation Awards [S49].
- Spread: Roblox itself featured it on its TikTok (499K+ likes), Instagram and X in late February 2025; creators Flamingo, KreekCraft, Thinknoodles and Sketch followed; 200K+ CCU then 600K+ the following weekend [S13]; Roblox's infrastructure post cites a 1M spike in March 2025 [S4]; peak 1,476,198 [S45]. GameAnalytics's takeaway: novelty plus familiarity, platform distribution, social proof, algorithmic feedback, repetition across TikTok, YouTube and the front page [S13].
- Aftermath: 31K 30-day peak by autumn 2026 [S45]; Roblox named Dead Rails a top-10 Robux earner in June 2025 [S8].

#### Fisch (WoozyNate, 2024-10-05)
- Built solo in about four months, inspired by Stardew Valley fishing; fish have a weight value that seeds about 25 mutation variants; 7 islands plus 2 hidden at launch; rods have unique abilities rather than a single best one; "Resilience" sets catch difficulty [S15].
- Growth: Halloween "FischFright" event pushed it to Roblox's front page; about 470K peak in late 2024 [S15]; Rolimons all-time 1,273,618 [S45]; Roblox's CEO said the algorithm promoted it in Indonesia [S38]. It became the top experience by CCU on 2024-11-30, passing Blox Fruits (fan wiki [S55]).
- Ownership timeline: Do Big Studios acquired it on 2024-11-02 (fan wiki [S55]; "November 2024" in trade press [S56]); on 2025-07-07 a Discord announcement said development had been handed back to some of the original developers, which Do Big did not confirm when asked, and Do Big still controlled the Roblox group at that time [S56]; the fan wiki says WoozyNate regained full control and the community on 2025-08-21 with co-manager Nick [S55]. WoozyNate had said in June 2025 the code had become hard to update [S56]. CCU fell from 539K (December 2024) to about 13K (July 2025) and then recovered to 1M+ on 2025-10-04 [S55][S56].
- Developer advice in the spotlight: a good trailer, moderators and Discord rules from day one, quality over quantity in updates and a steady cadence [S15]. Monetisation: cosmetics and starter packs [S15].

#### Dress to Impress (Gigi, 2023-11-11)
- Loop: a theme, 360 seconds to dress from a showroom, a runway where players rate 1-5 stars, podium for the top three; freeplay mode [S16]. 13 per server [S45].
- Team: in August 2024 Roblox's DevForum "Behind the Games" piece listed a nine-person team (three modelers, an animator, a builder, a hair modeler, a makeup artist, a scripter and a UI artist), who all worked on the 2024 "The Games" event, which hit 500K CCU on day one, the game's record at that time [S54]. A later Crossplay profile (undated) says about 30 developers, with full-time versus contractor split withheld, and names Umoyae FreeSpirit as director and general manager and Beatrice Elizabeth as community manager [S62]. Ownership structure and original build time are not disclosed in either source.
- Creator started at 14, 17 in 2024; story by M0t0Princess [S16]. Growth: "Pose 28" emote TikTok trend (spring 2024); streamers (Pokimane, Kai Cenat, CaseOh, James Charles, Madison Beer); the Charli XCX album collab in August 2024 reached 651K CCU; 2.7B visits and 250K+ average CCU by September 2024; Lady Gaga event August 2025; Wicked: For Good event November 2025 [S16]. Won Best Creative Direction, Best New Experience and Builderman Choice at the 2024 Innovation Awards [S16]. Rolimons later shows a 1.74M peak [S45]; social-media posts from mid-December 2024 report about 1.7M CCU during the winter update (snippet-level, not fetched) [S68], so the peak is a December 2024 event, not 2025.
- Monetisation: in-game currency, Robux items, limited codes [S16]. Controversy: late-2024 allegations against the creator [S16]. Seasonal items (Halloween, Christmas, Valentine's) are the update rhythm [S16].

#### Rivals, Forsaken, The Strongest Battlegrounds, DOORS (competitive/horror classics)
- RIVALS (Nosniy Games): 1v1 to 5v5 first-person shooter, first to 5 wins, 40 per server. Dates: the place was created 2024-05-26 and the public release is 2024-06-28 (a tracker and fan-wiki summaries [S18][S68]); both dates appear because they mark different events, but this is not confirmed by the developer. Peak 967,342 on 2026-02-14 (Rolimons, fetched [S45][S68]); 18.5B visits. Awards: Best Shooter at the 2025 [S49] and 2026 [S48] Innovation Awards, and Best Studio (Nosniy Games) in 2026 [S48]. Team: nosniy is the founder and creative director and SenseiWarrior the co-owner and lead programmer, with named map, music and 3D-art contributors (fan wiki [S68]); the studio dates from 2018 and Rivals began as a side project that moved from a PvE idea to a PvP shooter after a few months (fan wiki, unverified [S68]). Total headcount and build time are not disclosed. nosniy spoke on the GDC 2026 developer panel [S50].

## How to apply it (rules for an AI builder)
- DO choose a loop that can be explained in one sentence and shown in a 5-second clip: steal a thing, run from a wave, watch a number climb, spot the anomaly, survive the night. The 2025-26 hits all pass this test [S2][S28][S30][S35].
- DO size scope for a very small team: the verified hit teams are a teenager plus a studio (Grow a Garden v1, about 3 days), one developer (Fisch, about 4 months), three core people (99 Nights, about 3 months) and two core developers (DOORS); Roblox itself pitches "fewer than 10" [S1][S13][S15][S50][S66].
- DO make the first minute reward-dense: first purchase within about 30 seconds, first visible upgrade within 2 minutes, a second goal before the first ends (the Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot first sessions are all "buy, collect, buy better"). Average viral session is 10-16 minutes, so the first 3 minutes decide the session [S45].
- DO give the world a social conflict with a small server size: 4-8 players per server for steal/farm loops (Grow a Garden 4, Plants & Brainrots 5, Kick a Lucky Block 5, Steal An Egg 7, Steal a Brainrot 8); 20-30 only for co-op/horror/roleplay [S45].
- DO build passive income, offline progress and rebirth/prestige: Grow a Garden grows while you are away; Steal a Brainrot and Plants & Brainrots pay per second and reset for multipliers [S1][S2][S27].
- DO plan a weekly event cycle on day one (limited items, weather/boss events, "admin" drops). Grow a Garden's CCU stepped up with each weekly update (5M, 11.7M, 16M, 21.3M within five weeks) [S1].
- DO use a trending IP or meme only if you own or have licensed it; the Tung Tung Tung Sahur dispute shows the cost [S2]. A generic chase-and-collect theme avoids that.
- DO make rare items visible to other players (a leaderboard, a base display, an aura): rarity becomes a status symbol and fuels clips [S28].
- DO sell time compression and safety, not exclusive power gates: AFK treadmills (x9 and x100 Robux tiers) and shields/locks are the observed model [S2][S35]. Keep paid items clearly optional; Polygon criticised pay-to-win tiers [S2].
- DO add Roblox-native hooks: a trailer, strong thumbnail/icon, a Discord and moderator rules early (Fisch advice) [S15].
- DON'T plan around a single 22M viral peak. Every 2025-26 hit decayed 80-95% from peak within months (Steal a Brainrot 25.8M to a 1.7M 30-day peak; Grow a Garden to about 150K; Animal Hospital -62% in August) [S45][S31]. Plan the next two content drops and a sequel/second title.
- DON'T treat a sequel as inheriting players: Grow a Garden 2 peaked under 1M after the original reached 22M and fell 66% in a month [S32][S45].
- DON'T ship attention-extraction mechanics: a feed of short videos on a reward treadmill got Steal An Egg pulled and created a platform rule for Roblox Kids and Select [S31][S47].
- DON'T rely on bot or incentive-farmed counts: Roblox has been removing bot accounts since 2026-08-19; ranking ignores bot engagement [S36].
- DO optimise for retention not just visits: Roblox's recommendations are moving toward 28-day retention and playthrough rate [S10][S38].

## Recipes (each becomes a skill)

### Recipe 1: "Steal the item" base game (Steal a Brainrot / Steal An Egg)
When to use: the user wants a social-PvP incremental game with 6-8 players per server.
Steps:
1. Create a Workspace Folder `Bases` with N identical Models `Base1..BaseN` (N = server max, e.g. 8). Each Base has a `Floor` Part, a `Spawn` Part, slots `Slot1..Slot10` (anchored Parts or Attachments) and an `Door` Part (for the "locked" shield). Set Model Attribute `OwnerUserId = 0`.
2. On `Players.PlayerAdded`, assign the first free Base, set `OwnerUserId`, teleport the character to `Spawn`; on `PlayerRemoving` clear it and save data.
3. Build a conveyor in the map centre: a Part `ConveyorTrack` and a server Script that spawns a pet/item Model every 3-5 seconds from a weighted rarity table, moves it with a Tween or `AlignPosition` along the track, and removes it after 60 s. Each item has Attributes `Price`, `IncomePerSecond`, `Rarity`.
4. Buying: a `ProximityPrompt` on each conveyor item (`HoldDuration = 0`, `ActionText = "Buy"`). On `Triggered(player)` the server checks cash >= price, deducts, clones the item into the first free slot in that player's base.
5. Income: one server loop (`task.wait(1)`) adds `sum(IncomePerSecond)` to each owner's cash; show it in a `BillboardGui` over each item.
6. Stealing: each item in an unlocked base gets a second `ProximityPrompt` (`HoldDuration = 2`, `ActionText = "Steal"`, `MaxActivationDistance = 8`). On `Triggered(thief)` verify the thief is not the owner, the owner's base is not shielded, and the thief is within range; attach the item to the thief (a Weld to the character, speed -20%). If the thief reaches their own base `Spawn` region, transfer ownership; if killed or the owner re-captures, return it.
7. Shield: an owner `ProximityPrompt` on `Door` sets Attribute `LockedUntil = os.time() + 60`; the steal prompt is disabled while `os.time() < LockedUntil`; Robux product "extend lock" is optional.
8. Rebirth: a `ProximityPrompt` gated on cash >= 10x current rebirth cost; resets cash and slots, sets `Rebirths += 1`, raises a global multiplier by e.g. +50% per rebirth (Plants & Brainrots uses +50% Luck and Money per tier [S27]).
9. Persist cash, slots, rebirths with a DataStore (see file 04: UpdateAsync/ProfileStore).
Pitfalls: validate everything server side (distance, ownership, locks); never trust a client "steal" call; limit slot counts; rate-limit prompts; clean up welds on death/leave.

### Recipe 2: Offline-growth farm (Grow a Garden)
When to use: idle/farming game with plots and returning-player reward.
Steps:
1. Each player gets a plot Model (4 players per server fits the original).
2. Store for each plant: `plantedAt` (os.time), `growSeconds`, `typeId`, `mutation`. Do NOT tick growth every frame; compute `progress = (os.time() - plantedAt) / growSeconds` when displayed or harvested, so offline growth is free.
3. Seed shop with a stock that rotates on a timer (for example every 5 minutes) from a weighted table; stock shown to everyone.
4. Harvest by ProximityPrompt, sell at the sell NPC; rare seeds cost 10-100x more; show harvested fruit weight with a random size so rare outliers are bragging items.
5. Weather/event layer: a server timer picks an event (rain = growth x2, special mutation chance), announced with a UI banner.
6. Add pets later: egg hatching with a rarity table.
Pitfalls: store `os.time()` values, not elapsed counters; guard against clock edits (the server is the only source of time); cap offline stock to avoid infinite value.

### Recipe 3: Weekly live-event ("admin abuse") scheduler
When to use: any game that needs recurring spikes.
Steps:
1. Define an `Events` ModuleScript table: name, startsAt (UTC timestamp), duration, rewards, Attribute flags.
2. A server Script checks `os.time()` against the schedule every 10 s and sets Workspace Attributes (`ActiveEvent`); clients react via `GetAttributeChangedSignal`.
3. For a cross-server live moment (a boss, concert, or drop), publish via `MessagingService` so every server fires at once (see snippet).
4. Pre-announce in-game and on social channels 24 h ahead; limit exclusive items to online players during the window (Grow a Garden's weekly exclusives need presence [S1]).
5. Load-test the event; Roblox reports daily capacity stress tests for peaks [S4].
Pitfalls: MessagingService has rate limits; queue and de-duplicate by event id; keep events cosmetic-first so they do not break the economy.

### Recipe 4: Egg/pet collection with Luck stats (Pet Simulator 99, Steal An Egg, Plants & Brainrots)
When to use: any hatch-and-collect economy.
Steps: 1. Egg table `{pet, weight}` with 5-7 tiers; 2. a `Luck` stat multiplying weights above common; 3. hatch animation (camera + tween + particle) of 2-3 seconds, skippable by a gamepass; 4. pets give multipliers or income; 5. trading UI with a server-validated offer table; 6. limited event eggs.
Pitfalls: paid random items are regulated (file 08): show odds and check region rules; save pets as IDs not instances.

### Recipe 5: Speed incremental runner (+1 Speed Keyboard Escape)
When to use: a short-session, thumbnail-friendly incremental game.
Steps: 1. A long path of Parts; touching a "key" Part adds +1 to a `Speed` leaderstat (debounce per part); 2. `Humanoid.WalkSpeed = baseSpeed + Speed * step` (server-set; cap it); 3. 10-15 obstacle stages gated by required speed; 4. completing the course awards Wins; 5. Wins buy multipliers (trails/auras via cosmetic effects); 6. rebirth resets Speed for a permanent multiplier; 7. a free AFK treadmill plus Robux-tier treadmills (x9, x100) [S35]; 8. satisfying click sound on each step.
Pitfalls: WalkSpeed above roughly 100 breaks collision/streaming; ramp physics carefully; do not let AFK produce more than active play.

### Recipe 6: Co-op survival with day/night waves (99 Nights in the Forest)
When to use: 4-25 player co-op horror/survival.
Steps: 1. A central campfire Model with fuel value decreasing; 2. day phase: resource nodes (trees, scrap) respawn; 3. night phase: spawn enemies in waves scaled by `night number` and player count; 4. a countdown counter (night N of 99); 5. classes unlocked by badges/currency; 6. hidden children/NPC rescues as story beats; 7. weekly themed drops of monsters and classes [S26]; 8. a reward at the end of each night.
Pitfalls: server-side enemy AI cost; use `StreamingEnabled` and `task.wait` spawners; keep waves telegraphed so clips feel fair.

### Recipe 7: Anomaly-spotting shift horror (Animal Hospital)
When to use: low-art-cost horror with high replay.
Steps: 1. A shift timer (for example 8 in-game minutes); 2. patients arrive; the player matches a symptom to a medicine; 3. a hidden subset are anomalies with tells (wrong number of limbs, silhouettes, sounds); 4. a sanity meter drops on anomaly encounters and distorts the screen (ColorCorrectionEffect, vignette); 5. three deaths end the shift; 6. rewards per shift plus a permanent streak counter; 7. classes and consumables sold for earned coins or Robux [S30].
Pitfalls: balance fairness vs tension; keep 30-player servers moving with shared tasks.

### Recipe 8: Dress-up vote round (Dress to Impress)
When to use: a creative competitive social loop.
Steps: 1. Round state machine: lobby, theme reveal, 360 s dress phase, runway, voting 1-5, podium; 2. a showroom with categories (clothes, hair, accessories) using `HumanoidDescription` and `ApplyDescription`; 3. runway: each player walks a path while others rate through a UI (server accumulates, excludes self-votes); 4. rewards by placement; 5. monthly collaborations and seasonal items as the update rhythm [S16].
Pitfalls: moderate names/text; make voting robust against alt-account collusion.

### Recipe 9: Cultural-moment event (concerts and collabs)
When to use: a live event with a celebrity or IP partner.
Steps: 1. a stage Model and a timed cutscene; 2. a pre-recorded performance synced to a server clock so everyone sees it together (the Bruno Mars set was pre-recorded and under 8 minutes [S22]); 3. a limited collectible given to attendees (5.4M delivered [S21]); 4. rehearse capacity; 5. livestream alongside.
Pitfalls: Roblox must partner for licensed artists; keep the stage load light for mobile.

### Recipe 10: Hit-scouting and concept checklist
When to use: before committing to a concept.
Steps: score the idea 0-2 on each: (a) one-sentence loop, (b) shareable 5-second clip, (c) social conflict (steal/raid/vote), (d) offline or passive progress, (e) status item others can see, (f) weekly event hook, (g) servers of 4-8, (h) trend relevance, (i) legal clarity of IP, (j) retention beyond day 1. Build only concepts scoring 14+, and ship a playable vertical slice in 3-7 days (Grow a Garden v1 took three days [S1]).

```lua
--!strict
-- Weighted random pick (eggs, seeds, conveyor items)
local rng = Random.new()

local function weightedPick<T>(entries: { { item: T, weight: number } }, luck: number?): T
	local boost = luck or 1
	local total = 0
	for index, entry in entries do
		-- boost every entry except the first (common) one
		local w = if index == 1 then entry.weight else entry.weight * boost
		total += w
	end
	local roll = rng:NextNumber() * total
	for index, entry in entries do
		local w = if index == 1 then entry.weight else entry.weight * boost
		roll -= w
		if roll <= 0 then
			return entry.item
		end
	end
	return entries[#entries].item
end
```

local function growthProgress(plantedAt: number, growSeconds: number): number
	return math.clamp((os.time() - plantedAt) / growSeconds, 0, 1)
end

local function isReady(plantedAt: number, growSeconds: number): boolean
	return growthProgress(plantedAt, growSeconds) >= 1
end

```lua
--!strict
-- Server-validated steal prompt
local Players = game:GetService("Players")

local STEAL_RANGE = 12

local function wireStealPrompt(prompt: ProximityPrompt, item: Model)
	prompt.ActionText = "Steal"
	prompt.HoldDuration = 2
	prompt.MaxActivationDistance = 8
	prompt.Triggered:Connect(function(thief: Player)
		local ownerId = item:GetAttribute("OwnerUserId")
		if ownerId == thief.UserId then
			return
		end
		local lockedUntil = item:GetAttribute("LockedUntil") :: number?
		if lockedUntil and os.time() < lockedUntil then
			return
		end
		local root = thief.Character and thief.Character:FindFirstChild("HumanoidRootPart")
		if not root or not root:IsA("BasePart") then
			return
		end
		if (root.Position - item:GetPivot().Position).Magnitude > STEAL_RANGE then
			return
		end
		item:SetAttribute("CarriedBy", thief.UserId)
	end)
end
```

```lua
--!strict
-- Cross-server live event with MessagingService (rate limits apply)
local MessagingService = game:GetService("MessagingService")
local TOPIC = "LiveEvent"

local function broadcastEvent(eventId: string, startsAtUnix: number)
	local ok, err = pcall(function()
		MessagingService:PublishAsync(TOPIC, { id = eventId, startsAt = startsAtUnix })
	end)
	if not ok then
		warn("publish failed:", err)
	end
end

local handled: { [string]: boolean } = {}
MessagingService:SubscribeAsync(TOPIC, function(message)
	local data = message.Data
	if handled[data.id] then
		return
	end
	handled[data.id] = true
	task.delay(math.max(0, data.startsAt - os.time()), function()
		workspace:SetAttribute("ActiveEvent", data.id)
	end)
end)
```

-- Conveyor item moved with a tween along a straight track
local TweenService = game:GetService("TweenService")

local function runAlongTrack(item: BasePart, startCF: CFrame, endCF: CFrame, seconds: number)
	item.CFrame = startCF
	local tween = TweenService:Create(item, TweenInfo.new(seconds, Enum.EasingStyle.Linear), { CFrame = endCF })
	tween.Completed:Once(function()
		item:Destroy()
	end)
	tween:Play()
end
