# Game 1, round 3: blind critique

**1. Verdict**

This is a bare prototype: a blank baseplate with four empty brown pads, three colored squares and one tiny crystal. It shows nothing of mining, pickaxe upgrades or caves, and it would be abandoned within seconds.

**2. Scores (0 to 10)**

| Area | Score |
|---|---|
| Delivers the idea | 1 |
| World and level design | 1 |
| Art direction (palette, materials, lighting, atmosphere) | 1.5 |
| Crystal and prop quality | 2 |
| UI/UX | 4 |
| Feedback and game feel | 1 |
| 10-second clarity | 2 |
| **Overall** | **1.5** |

**3. Flaws, most severe first**

1. **There is no mining in any screenshot (all four).**
   - I see no pickaxe, no player avatar, no ore nodes, no crystal clusters to hit, no cave entrance and no depth layers.
   - The whole premise is missing. The only mineable-looking object is one small crystal on the hub.
   - It should be a mining area with dozens of glowing crystal nodes, a visible pickaxe in hand, and a tunnel leading down into deeper caves.

2. **The four plots are empty (03, 01).**
   - In 03 the plot is just a tiled brown deck with a "Plot" sign on it. No crystals, no tools and no cave mouth.
   - In 01 all four are identical empty brown squares on thin tan strips.
   - They should be distinct mine entrances or zones, each with a tier name, a depth number and a visible reward.

3. **There is no onboarding (all four).**
   - I see no objective text, no arrow, no highlighted first target, no prompt such as "Mine this" and no tooltip.
   - A new player has no idea what to do. The only cues are three unlabeled colored squares and a HUD counter stuck at 0.
   - It should open with a pulsing arrow to the first crystal and a "Hit it!" prompt, with the first reward inside 10 seconds.

4. **The world is a floating tabletop in a void (01, 03, 04).**
   - In 01 the hub and plots sit on an endless flat gray checkered plane with no terrain, no cliffs and no rock.
   - In 03 a hard-edged rectangular hole of gray checker cuts into the grass.
   - In 04 the green ends in a straight line against a gray flat sea.
   - A mining game needs a mountain, quarry or cavern. The ground should enclose the player in rock, not float on nothing.

5. **The environment is inconsistent between shots (01 versus 02–04).**
   - 01 has a flat gray, hazy sky and no grass.
   - 02–04 show bright blue sky and green grass patches.
   - Either the world streams in unevenly or the lighting is not set. Neither is acceptable at launch.
   - It should be one deliberate, always-loaded environment, dark and moody for a cave game.

6. **The materials are default Roblox baseplate (02, 03, 04).**
   - The hub is the white studded baseplate.
   - The plots are brown studded tile.
   - The grass is the flat default green.
   - Studs on every surface read as "unmodified template". The ground should be rock, crystal-veined stone and packed dirt with real textures, normal detail and variation.

7. **The crystal is far too small and too lonely (02, 04).**
   - In 02 it is a single small purple and blue spike cluster, about the size of a person's torso, lost in a huge white field.
   - In 04 it is a tiny speck.
   - It has no visible glow, bloom or light cast. The star-shaped shadow under it is harsh and dark, which is the opposite of a glowing object.
   - Crystals should be large, clustered and emissive, with bloom, colored light on the surrounding rock, particles and a rarity color scheme.

8. **The lighting and atmosphere are flat daylight (all four).**
   - There is hard sun, a pale sky and no fog, no ambient color grade and no point lights.
   - "Glowing crystals" require darkness for the glow to read.
   - It should be a dim cave with colored crystal light, depth fog, bloom and color correction.

9. **The floating labels collide and misplace (04, 02).**
   - In 04 "SHOP" and "REBIRTH" overlap and read as "SHOPREBIRTH".
   - In 02 the giant "SHOP" text hangs in the air to the right of the green pad, not clearly attached to it.
   - The blue pad has no visible label at all.
   - Labels should sit one per pad, spaced apart, scaled by distance and consistent. They need clear icons and a color match to the pad.

10. **The hub layout is unreadable and lopsided (01, 02, 04).**
    - The pads are plain flat colored rectangles (blue, green, purple) of uneven size, scattered at the corner of a big white square.
    - There are no stations, machines, NPCs or props. Nothing says "shop" or "rebirth".
    - It should have a designed hub with a shop building or vendor, an upgrade anvil or forge and a rebirth altar. Its silhouette should be recognizable from far away.

11. **The scale is wrong (01, 02, 03).**
    - In 01 the hub and plots are tiny, so the player would cross huge empty gaps over thin tan strips.
    - In 02 the distant plot is a long way from the hub.
    - Travel is slow and dead, with nothing to see on the way. A tight, dense layout should put the first mine within a few seconds' walk.

12. **The HUD shows idle numbers without game context (all four).**
    - "CRYSTALS +0/s" implies an idle game, but nothing in the world produces crystals. The counter sits at 0 in every shot.
    - The currency icon is a generic "C" coin, not a crystal.
    - The HUD should show a crystal icon, a clear click-to-mine loop and a per-click value. A pickaxe and tier indicator should sit next to it.

13. **The side buttons are redundant and cramped (all four).**
    - SHOP and UPGRADES sit on the HUD, and a SHOP pad sits in the world.
    - "UPGRADES" runs almost edge to edge in its button, with the arrow icon crowding the text.
    - The REBIRTH button is greyed out with an empty progress bar and no number, so the player does not know what unlocks it or how far away it is.
    - It should show a requirement such as "Reach 10,000 crystals" and a progress fraction.

14. **The text styling is inconsistent (04, 02, 03).**
    - The world labels are big white text with a heavy outline, while the "Plot" sign is a small studded wooden sign with a different font weight.
    - There is no unified typeface or icon language between the HUD and the world.

15. **There is no visible feedback of any kind (all four).**
    - I see no hit sparks, no damage numbers, no crystal-break effect, no screen shake, no collectible pop and no coin-fly to the HUD.
    - Mining games live on this feedback loop. Every swing needs sound, particles, a number pop and a counter bump.

16. **There is no progression or depth cue (01, 03).**
    - I see no sign of depth levels, tier names, unlock gates or a minimap. The only cues are a "Plot" sign and a locked button.
    - "Deeper caves" should be visible as a descending tunnel or stairs, with depth markers and locked gates showing the required rebirth count.

17. **The shadows are crude (01, 02).**
    - The star-shaped shadow under the crystal is a dark hard blob that reads as an unfinished decal.
    - The tiny floating dark shapes beside the plots in 01 look like stray artifacts, and I cannot tell what they are.

**4. What a top-studio version looks like**

- You spawn at the mouth of a mine in a dark cavern. The lighting is moody and colored crystal light spills across rock walls.
- A glowing amethyst and cyan cluster sits two steps ahead, and an arrow pulses over it. Your pickaxe is already in hand.
- One click gives a satisfying crack, shards fly, a "+5" floats up and flies to a crystal-icon counter that bounces.
- Beside it are the forge for upgrades, a vendor and a rebirth altar. Each one is a real model with a billboard and a price.
- A glowing tunnel descends, gated by depth and rebirth number with a visible progress bar.
- Rarer crystals appear deeper in escalating colors, with audio and screen effects. A leaderboard and pet or aura slots round out the loop.

**5. What is already good**

- The HUD has a consistent chunky, studded, rounded style with readable bold text and good contrast (all four).
- The currency panel is in a conventional top-left position, with a buy-more "+" button.
- The Shop, Upgrades and Rebirth buttons have distinct color coding (green, blue, purple) and clear icons.
- The Rebirth button is honestly shown as locked.
- The one crystal has a pleasant purple-to-cyan gradient, a faceted spiky silhouette and a hint of translucency (02).
- The three hub pads use a coherent three-color scheme that matches the HUD buttons.
- The hub is symmetrical, with four plots connected by paths. The structure is easy to grasp from above (01).
